## Why this exists

Secretly is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3, and the same
code is also offered under a separate commercial licence to organisations that
cannot use AGPL. Revenue from those commercial licences is what funds the
project.

Offering a commercial licence is only lawful if one party holds sufficient rights
to the whole codebase. This agreement gives the Rights Holder those rights
**without taking anything away from you**: you keep the copyright in your contribution and
remain free to use it anywhere else, for any purpose.

If you would rather not grant these rights, please say so before you write code —
we would rather discuss it early than turn down work you have already done.

## How to sign

Open a pull request and leave a comment containing exactly this text, with your
own details filled in:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.

Name:    <your full name>
Email:   <your email>
GitHub:  <your username>
Date:    <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

That comment, together with your commit history, is the record of your agreement.
One signature covers all of your future contributions — you do not need to repeat
it for every pull request.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, ask them to email
**support@secretlyapp.com** confirming they permit it, before you sign.
